Who We Are

Uganda's trusted partner in environmental management

MSITU Consults Limited is a leading local provider of Environmental and Social Management consultancy, established in 2011 and headquartered at Kyambogo Banda, Kampala. We are NEMA-certified, URA tax compliant, and registered with PPDA.

Our activities are managed by highly qualified environmentalists, physical planners, sociologists, civil engineers, surveyors, property valuers, public health experts, archaeologists and economists, with access to over 40 external associate consultants.
