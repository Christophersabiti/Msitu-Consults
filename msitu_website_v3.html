Who we are

A Ugandan consultancy for environmental and social management

MSITU Consults Limited is a Kampala-based provider of environmental and social management consultancy, established in 2011 and headquartered at Kyambogo Banda. We support government institutions, development partners, private developers and NGOs from project scoping through to assessment, implementation and monitoring.

Our work is led by in-house specialists and supported by a network of over 40 associate consultants, giving clients multidisciplinary coverage across Uganda and the wider region.

We apply national environmental law alongside international frameworks such as the World Bank Environmental and Social Standards and IFC Performance Standards where projects require it.

Insights & regulatory guidance

What environmental approvals might your project need?

Under Uganda's National Environment Act, 2019, many developments need an environmental and social assessment before they proceed. The type of study depends on the project's nature, scale and location. Here is a short guide to the main instruments.

Screening

Project brief

An initial document describing the project and its likely effects. It helps determine whether a full assessment is needed.

Assessment

ESIA

A full study of environmental and social impacts covering scoping, baseline, consultation, impact analysis and mitigation, submitted for regulatory review.

Management

ESMP

An Environmental and Social Management Plan setting out who will do what, when and how to manage impacts during construction and operation.

Compliance

Environmental audit

A periodic check of an operating facility's performance against its approval conditions and the law.

MSITU is an independent consultancy. Approvals and certificates are issued by the regulator, not by MSITU, and review timelines are set by the regulator. For official requirements, see the National Environment Management Authority (NEMA) (opens in a new tab).
